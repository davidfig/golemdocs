## Fonts

## LAME (MP3 encoder)

The client depends on `wasm-media-encoders` 0.7.0 (MIT, © 2020–2024 arseneyr), whose `mp3.wasm` is [LAME](https://lame.sourceforge.io/) 3.100 compiled to WebAssembly without its decoder. The package says only MIT; LAME is under the GNU Library General Public License, version 2 or later (`gain_analysis.c`: the Lesser GPL, 2.1 or later; `libmp3lame/fft.c` has its author's own note instead).

- The license and the complete source are in `client/public/licenses/lame/`, and on the website at `/licenses/lame/`: `COPYING.txt`, LAME at commit `98db548e8e851defbba3184125ce10725355c332`, and the package's own C file and build script at its published commit (`4a45333baadbab312d1cc0911151dfad23157c51`).
- `mp3.wasm` must be served as an unmodified file of its own, never inlined, so that another build of LAME can be put in its place. It is fetched when an edited MP3 is saved (docs/audio-editing.md), and the welcome document credits LAME with links to its site and license.

`@swc/helpers` (Apache-2.0), a dependency of the package, is not in the module build the client uses.

## npm dependencies

None of the npm dependencies end up in what ships to users except `libheif-js` and `polygon-clipping` in the client bundle, and `wasm-media-encoders` (above), fetched when an edited MP3 is saved. Their licenses are in their packages under `node_modules`.
