Free to use. Subscribe for sync, sharing, and AI.

Golem Docs works fully without an account. Syncing your documents across devices, sharing them, and the AI assistant need a subscription: $5 a month. Cancel any time.

  • Free, with no accountEdit, convert, and download as much as you like. Documents are saved in your browser and never sent to us.
  • With a subscriptionYour documents follow you to any device. Share a link or invite people to edit together, live. Use the AI assistant to read and edit your document. Sign in with Google, Microsoft, GitHub, or Apple to subscribe.
  • Opening what others share is freeIf a subscriber shares a document with you, you only need to sign in to open it, and to edit it if they let you.
  • If you cancelYour documents stay in your browser, and downloading a file never needs an account or a subscription.
