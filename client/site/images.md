# Edit pictures in your browser

Drop a PNG, JPEG, or WebP onto Golem Docs and it opens as a picture you can crop, correct, mark up, and save. Nothing is uploaded, so screenshots and photos stay on your device.

## Open a picture, or put it in a document

Drop an image onto the window and Golem Docs asks what to do with it: insert it in the document you are working on, or open it as a picture of its own. GIF, BMP, and SVG files open as pictures too, and save as PNG.

## Fix and reshape

The Picture menu has the everyday tools.

- **Rotate and flip:** rotate left, right, or 180 degrees, and flip horizontally or vertically.
- **Crop:** drag the handles for a free crop, keep the original proportions, or crop to a shape.
- **Resize:** set the width and height in pixels, and keep the proportions if you like.
- **Adjust colors:** brightness, contrast, and saturation, with a reset.
- **Auto Levels:** one click to even out a dull photo.
- **Effects:** grayscale, sepia, invert colors, blur, and sharpen.

## Mark it up

The Draw toolbar turns a picture into an annotated one. It is made for screenshots.

- Pen and highlighter for freehand marks.
- Line, arrow, rectangle, and ellipse, with an option to fill shapes.
- Text and speech bubbles.
- Numbered markers, for walking through steps.
- Move to reposition something you drew, and eraser to take it away.
- A color picker and four line thicknesses, from thin to extra thick.

Undo steps back through your changes.

## Hide sensitive details

The **Redact** tool pixelates part of a picture, which is handy for names, numbers, and faces in a screenshot.

## Save in the format you need

Download as PNG for a lossless copy, JPEG for a smaller photo, or WebP for the web. JPEG and WebP compress, so they trade a little detail for size. You can also put a picture on a page: convert it to Word or PDF and it becomes a page with the picture on it, with nothing lost.

Going the other way, [converting a document](/home/convert) to PNG, JPEG, or WebP makes a picture of its pages.
