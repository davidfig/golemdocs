# Open huge text and log files

Most editors choke on a 50 MB log file. Golem Docs opens it. Files of 8 MB or more open in a viewer built for the size, so you can scroll, search, filter, and copy without freezing your browser.

## What opens this way

Plain text, log files, CSV, and TSV files of 8 MB or more. Smaller files open as ordinary documents you can edit.

## Built for size

- **Instant to open.** A line index means the first lines appear right away instead of after the whole file has loaded. In our tests, a 51 MB log with 500,000 lines showed its first lines in about a tenth of a second.
- **Smooth scrolling.** Only the lines on screen are drawn, however long the file is.
- **Search and filter.** Find every match, or filter the view down to just the lines that contain your text.
- **Go to line.** Jump straight to a line number.
- **Select and copy.** Select lines and copy them.
- **Level colors.** Lines in a log are colored by level, so errors and warnings stand out.
- **Wrapped lines.** Wrap long lines, or turn wrapping off with Alt+Z or the button in the status bar.
- **Text size.** Make the text bigger or smaller.
- **Download the original.** Get the exact bytes back.

## Private

The file is read in your browser and stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Good to know

The large file viewer is for reading and searching. Editing and saving a file this size isn't available yet. For a smaller file, or to work on part of a log, use [an ordinary document](/home/documents) or [convert it](/home/convert).
