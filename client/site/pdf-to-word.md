# Convert a PDF to Word without uploading it

Open a PDF in Golem Docs, choose File ▸ Convert To ▸ Word, and you get an editable .docx made from it. The conversion runs in your browser, so the PDF is never uploaded to a server, and you don't need an account.

## How to do it

1. **Open the PDF.** Drop it onto the Golem Docs window, or choose File ▸ Open.
2. **Choose File ▸ Convert To ▸ Word.** The PDF becomes a Word document you can edit like any other.
3. **Download it.** Choose File ▸ Download to get the .docx.

## What comes across

- **The text,** kept where the PDF had it, with headings in bold and the text's color.
- **Pictures,** including ones that are turned or mirrored, and black-and-white fax scans.
- **Vector drawings,** such as logos, charts, line art, and QR codes, kept as sharp pictures that stay clear however far you zoom.
- **Running headers and footers,** such as dates, titles, and page numbers, which become the document's header and footer.

## What to know

- **It is a reflowed copy.** The text is yours to edit, but the page layout won't match the original exactly. To change a PDF and keep it a PDF, use [Edit PDF](/home/pdf).
- **Scanned pages are pictures.** A PDF made by scanning paper has no text to convert, so its pages come across as pictures.

To go the other way, or to turn a PDF into Markdown, HTML, or another format, see [converting documents](/home/convert).
