# Edit vector drawings in your browser

Open an SVG or compressed SVG (.svgz) in Golem Docs and it opens as a drawing you can edit: draw shapes, reshape paths, add gradients and brush strokes, and save it as SVG again. Nothing is uploaded, so your artwork stays on your device.

## A drawing, not a picture

SVG files are not pictures made of pixels, so they have a kind of their own, listed under Vector in the format list. A drawing stays sharp at any zoom, and saving it again keeps everything the file had, including parts the editor does not change, such as filters.

## Draw and select

The Drawing menu and toolbar have the tools.

- **Select:** click to pick a shape, Shift to add more, or drag a box around several. Drag to move, use the handles to resize, and the round handle to rotate; hold Shift to keep proportions or turn in 15 degree steps.
- **Shapes:** rectangle, ellipse, and line.
- **Pen:** click for corners, drag for smooth curves, and click the first point to close the path.
- **Text:** click anywhere and type.
- **Edit points:** double-click a path to move its points and curve handles, add a point on the line, or delete one.

## Color, gradients, and strokes

- **Fill and outline:** pick a color for each, or none, and set the outline width.
- **Gradients:** choose a linear or radial fill, then drag its points and stops right on the drawing. Double-click the line to add a stop.
- **Stroke styles:** dashes, dots, and arrowheads; lines that taper or swell; calligraphic pens; dry brush and chalk; patterns repeated along a path; and scattered shapes such as stars and confetti. A stroke keeps its path, so you can reshape the line afterwards.

## Arrange

- **Layers:** a panel lists every shape. Hide or lock one, rename it, or drag it to a new place in the order or into a group.
- **Group, order, and duplicate:** bring shapes to the front, send them back, or copy them.
- **Combine shapes:** unite, intersect, subtract, or exclude to make one shape from several.
- **Clipping masks:** cut shapes to the outline of the one on top.
- **Snapping:** shapes snap to each other and to the page edges, with guide lines. Hold Alt to turn it off for a moment.

Undo and redo step through every change.

## Convert it

A drawing converts like any other document. To PDF it stays vector, with its curves, gradients, and clips. To PNG, JPEG, or WebP it becomes a picture at full resolution, and in a Word file it becomes a page with the picture on it. Going the other way, any document can become SVG: its text stays as text, in place, so you can still edit the words. See [what each conversion keeps](/home/convert).

## Ask Clip

Clip, the assistant, can read a drawing and change it for you: add shapes, recolor them, group and arrange them, combine them, and apply gradients and strokes. Each change is one step you can undo.
